ABOUT ME

Dedicated to
serving the people

Namaste! I am Ramadhar Bhandary, a committed social worker and currently serving as the Adhyaksh (President) of Nepal Phool Mali Samaj.

I have been working tirelessly for the betterment of people in Ward No. 2, Mahottari. From supporting flower growers and farmers to organizing community programs and helping the needy, my journey has always been about जनसेवा (public service).

Current Roles

  • Adhyaksh — Nepal Phool Mali Samaj
  • Social Worker — Ward No. 2, Mahottari
  • Active member of local development initiatives
MY COMMITMENT

Work for the People

Real work. Real impact. Serving the community of Mahottari with dedication.

Supporting Flower Growers

As President of Nepal Phool Mali Samaj, I actively support local flower farmers with resources, training, and market access.

Community Development

Organized multiple adhiveshan, health camps, and awareness programs for the welfare of people in Ward No. 2 and surrounding areas.

Helping the Needy

Regular support to poor families, elderly citizens, and students through education aid, food distribution, and emergency help.
